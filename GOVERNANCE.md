# Governance

AIC Aegis uses policy-as-code, evidence packs, runtime traceability, and eval gates to govern AI behavior.

## Governance controls

- ADRs for architectural decisions.
- Epics and work packets for execution.
- Policy packs for runtime authorization.
- Eval packs for quality and safety regression testing.
- Tool manifests for capabilities and risk metadata.
- Evidence packs for auditability.
