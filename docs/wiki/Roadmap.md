# Roadmap

MVP-A, MVP-B, v1, and v2.

---

## Purpose

The Roadmap defines phased product evolution.

---

## MVP-A — Proof Loop

Prove:

* governed run,
* tool proposal,
* policy decision,
* high-risk block or approval gate,
* Evidence Pack,
* run timeline.

---

## MVP-B — Learning Loop

Add:

* governed memory,
* Memory Admission Gate,
* feedback,
* eval runner,
* business outcome records.

---

## v1

Add:

* stronger tenant model,
* real adapters,
* policy packs,
* evidence exports,
* SDKs,
* admin UI,
* cloud-native deployment.

---

## v2

Add:

* advanced evals,
* trust dashboards,
* connector registry,
* autonomy ladder,
* enterprise governance.

---

## North Star

The roadmap should increase trust before increasing autonomy.
