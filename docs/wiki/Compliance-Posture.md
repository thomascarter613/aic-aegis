# Compliance Posture

What is in scope and out of scope.

---

## Purpose

Compliance Posture states what Aegis does and does not claim.

---

## MVP Position

MVP is not production HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2, or regulated-grade.

MVP is governance-oriented and evidence-first, but not certified.

---

## Future Direction

Future Aegis may support:

* audit exports,
* retention policies,
* access controls,
* evidence redaction,
* compliance mapping,
* control coverage.

---

## Rule

Do not claim compliance without controls and evidence.

---

## North Star

Compliance posture must be honest.
