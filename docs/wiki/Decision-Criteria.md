# Decision Criteria

How Aegis decisions should be made.

---

## Purpose

Decision Criteria define how Aegis chooses between options.

---

## Criteria

Prefer decisions that:

1. preserve Aegis Laws,
2. strengthen governance,
3. improve evidence,
4. keep domain core clean,
5. support local-first MVP,
6. reduce unnecessary complexity,
7. improve testability,
8. support future cloud-native path,
9. avoid vendor lock-in,
10. improve measurable business outcomes.

---

## Rejection Criteria

Reject choices that:

* bypass Tool Broker,
* bypass Memory Admission Gate,
* skip policy,
* skip evidence,
* create silent failure,
* weaken tenant isolation,
* add premature complexity.

---

## North Star

Decision Criteria keep Aegis aligned with its mission.
