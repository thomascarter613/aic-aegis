## Purpose

## First Demo

The first demo is the Governed Sales/Ops Assistant.

It should show:

1. AI summarizes a synthetic customer conversation.
2. AI drafts follow-up.
3. AI proposes CRM update.
4. AI proposes email send.
5. Aegis allows safe work.
6. Aegis blocks or approval-gates risky work.
7. Aegis generates evidence.
8. Aegis records outcome.

---

## Demo Message

> Aegis lets AI do useful work while keeping risky actions governed.
