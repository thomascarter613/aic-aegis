# Webhook Strategy

Inbound and outbound webhooks later.

---

## Purpose

Webhook Strategy defines how Aegis may receive and send events.

---

## Future Webhooks

Inbound:

* workflow triggers,
* feedback submission,
* business system events.

Outbound:

* run completed,
* approval requested,
* evidence generated,
* eval failed,
* outcome recorded.

---

## Rule

Webhook activity must be tenant-scoped and policy-aware.

---

## North Star

Webhooks should connect Aegis to workflows without bypassing governance.
