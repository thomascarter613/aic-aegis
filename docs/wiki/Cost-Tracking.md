# Cost Tracking

Model, tool, runtime, and infrastructure cost records.

---

## Purpose

Cost Tracking helps Aegis measure the cost of AI work.

---

## Cost Areas

* model tokens,
* model calls,
* tool calls,
* runtime duration,
* storage,
* evidence generation,
* eval runs,
* infrastructure.

---

## Cost per Outcome

Long term, Aegis should compare cost to outcomes.

Example:

* cost per useful draft,
* cost per risk prevented,
* cost per verified hour saved.

---

## North Star

Cost Tracking helps prove AI work is profitable, not just possible.
