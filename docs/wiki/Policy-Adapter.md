# Policy Adapter

OPA/Rego and mock policy adapters.

---

## Purpose

Policy Adapter connects Aegis to policy engines.

---

## Adapter Options

* mock policy adapter,
* OPA/Rego adapter,
* future policy engines.

---

## Rule

The domain core depends on policy decision abstractions, not OPA internals.

---

## MVP Scope

MVP-A can use mock policy.

OPA can be added when useful.

---

## North Star

Policy adapters make runtime governance executable and replaceable.
