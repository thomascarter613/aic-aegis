# Product Decision Records

Product-level decisions.

---

## Purpose

Product Decision Records preserve important product choices.

---

## What Belongs Here

Use Product Decision Records for decisions about:

* target users,
* MVP scope,
* pricing direction later,
* market category,
* non-goals,
* user workflows,
* success metrics,
* roadmap sequencing.

---

## Record Format

Each record should include:

* title,
* status,
* date,
* context,
* decision,
* consequences,
* alternatives,
* related docs.

---

## North Star

Product Decision Records prevent product direction from being lost or repeatedly relitigated.
