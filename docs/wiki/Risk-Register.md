# Risk Register

Known risks and mitigations.

---

## Purpose

Risk Register tracks risks, severity, mitigations, owners, and status.

---

## Initial Risks

| Risk                     | Severity | Mitigation                        |
| ------------------------ | -------- | --------------------------------- |
| Direct tool execution    | High     | Tool Broker required              |
| Unmanaged memory         | High     | Memory Admission Gate             |
| Missing evidence         | High     | Evidence Pack required            |
| Policy unavailable       | High     | Fail closed for high-risk actions |
| Cross-tenant leakage     | Critical | Tenant-scoped records             |
| Business value overclaim | Medium   | Outcome records required          |
| Scope creep              | Medium   | MVP-A focus                       |

---

## North Star

The Risk Register makes known risks visible and manageable.
