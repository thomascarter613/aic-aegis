# Auditability

What must be recorded and why.

---

## Purpose

Auditability means Aegis can explain what happened.

---

## Must Record

* run identity,
* run events,
* model calls,
* tool proposals,
* policy decisions,
* approvals,
* evidence,
* evals,
* feedback,
* outcomes,
* failures.

---

## Why

Auditability supports:

* debugging,
* governance,
* client trust,
* compliance readiness,
* regression prevention,
* outcome verification.

---

## North Star

Auditability turns AI behavior into reviewable history.
