# Identity and Access Model

Users, tenants, agents, roles, permissions.

---

## Purpose

Identity and Access Model defines who can do what in Aegis.

---

## Core Identities

* tenant,
* user,
* agent,
* workflow,
* tool,
* policy,
* approver,
* system actor.

---

## Core Permissions

Potential permissions:

* run:create,
* run:read,
* tool:propose,
* tool:execute,
* approval:decide,
* memory:admit,
* evidence:export,
* policy:manage.

---

## MVP Scope

MVP may use simple placeholder identity but must preserve fields.

---

## North Star

Identity makes AI action attributable.
