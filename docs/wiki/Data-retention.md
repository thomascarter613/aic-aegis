# Data Retention

Evidence, events, memory, feedback, and outcomes.

---

## Purpose

Data Retention defines how long Aegis keeps records.

---

## Retention Areas

* run events,
* Evidence Packs,
* memory,
* feedback,
* eval results,
* outcomes,
* approvals,
* policy decisions.

---

## MVP Scope

MVP may use simple retention.

Future versions should support retention policies by tenant and data class.

---

## North Star

Retention should preserve auditability without keeping unsafe data forever.
