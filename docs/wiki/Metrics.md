# Metrics

Reliability, safety, eval, cost, and outcome metrics.

---

## Purpose

Metrics measure Aegis behavior.

---

## Metric Areas

* run completion rate,
* failure rate,
* policy decisions,
* high-risk blocks,
* approval count,
* evidence completeness,
* eval pass rate,
* feedback score,
* outcomes recorded,
* estimated time saved,
* cost per run.

---

## North Star

Metrics help Aegis improve reliability and business value.
