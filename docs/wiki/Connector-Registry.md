# Connector-Registry

Future connector catalog.

---

## Purpose

Connector Registry defines future catalog of integrations.

---

## Future Connectors

Possible connectors:

* CRM,
* email,
* docs,
* tickets,
* calendar,
* storage,
* observability,
* policy engines,
* model providers.

---

## Registry Fields

Each connector should define:

* connector ID,
* status,
* owner,
* auth model,
* tool manifests,
* risk classes,
* policy requirements.

---

## North Star

Connector Registry makes integrations discoverable and governable.
