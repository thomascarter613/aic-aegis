# Acceptance Criteria Standards

What “done” means at task/work-packet level.

---

## Purpose

Acceptance Criteria Standards define how work is judged complete.

---

## Good Acceptance Criteria

Good criteria are:

* specific,
* testable,
* observable,
* linked to files or behavior,
* aligned with Aegis Laws,
* clear enough for AI agents.

---

## Required Criteria Areas

Each work packet should define:

* functional acceptance,
* architecture acceptance,
* test acceptance,
* documentation acceptance,
* evidence or audit acceptance where relevant.

---

## North Star

Acceptance criteria prevent vague completion claims.
