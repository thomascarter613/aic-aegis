# Maintainer-Guide

How to steward the project.

---

## Purpose

Maintainer Guide defines how Aegis should be cared for over time.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

* preserve doctrine,
* review PRs,
* maintain roadmap,
* update wiki,
* manage issues,
* record decisions,
* protect security,
* keep MVP focused.

---

## North Star

Maintainers protect the meaning of Aegis.
